# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of Hermes Theme Bento pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone — regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to building a community that actively welcomes diverse perspectives and lived experiences. This includes a specific commitment to accessibility: we build software that anyone can use, and we hold ourselves to that same standard in how we communicate and collaborate.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### Behavior we encourage

- Demonstrating empathy and genuine kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving feedback that is constructive, specific, and kind — and receiving it with the same spirit
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from the experience
- Prioritizing what is best for the community as a whole, not just individual preferences
- Welcoming contributors of all experience levels — a thoughtful question from a newcomer is as valuable as a patch from a veteran
- Centering accessibility in both code reviews and community discussion

### Behavior that is not acceptable

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment of any form
- Publishing another person's private information — such as a physical address, email address, or other identifying details — without their explicit permission
- Dismissing, mocking, or belittling accessibility concerns or the needs of users with disabilities
- Sustained disruption of community discussions, reviews, or events
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Accessibility as a Community Value

This project builds UI for humans. That means every contributor — regardless of ability, device, or context — deserves to be considered. We extend that same value to how we treat each other in this community.

If you notice that a discussion, process, or decision unintentionally excludes someone, please say so. We want to know.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they determine to be inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct. Moderation decisions will be communicated with reasoning where appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces — GitHub Issues, Discussions, Pull Requests, Discord, and any other official venue. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces (e.g., at a conference, in a blog post, or on social media in an official capacity).

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it as soon as possible.

**How to report:**

- **Email:** conduct@hermes-theme.dev *(monitored by maintainers)*
- **GitHub:** Open a private security advisory via the repository's Security tab if you prefer a confidential channel

All reports will be reviewed promptly and investigated fairly. The privacy and security of the reporter will be respected throughout the process. We will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

---

## Enforcement

Upon receiving a report, maintainers will:

1. Acknowledge receipt within 48 hours.
2. Review the incident and gather additional context if needed.
3. Determine an appropriate response, which may include:
   - A private written warning with explanation of the violation
   - A temporary suspension from community participation
   - A permanent ban from the community for severe or repeated violations

All decisions will be communicated to the reporter. Appeals may be submitted by replying to the enforcement communication.
